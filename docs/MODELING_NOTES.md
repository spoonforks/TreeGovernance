# Modelling notes

The map is for discovery and system understanding, not a formal legal model.

The working lifecycle is:

```text
policy → planning → site/species design → planting → establishment
→ inspection → assessment → management decision
→ maintenance or permit route → intervention → replacement
→ monitoring / feedback
```

## Documented vs synthesis
- **documented**: directly described by cited material at roughly the represented level.
- **synthesis**: an analytical bridge assembled from evidence and domain logic.

## Lenses
Lenses are curated subgraphs. Avoid unexplained orphan nodes; include bridge processes and relations needed to preserve meaningful pathways.

## Current research gaps
Procurement and contract governance, utilities, provincial/water-board interfaces, private-tree governance, and stronger links between biodiversity monitoring and operational decisions.
